# Arbitrage Monkey — Product Overview

## Product Summary
Arbitrage Monkey is a user-friendly platform that continuously scans supported crypto exchanges for price differences, surfacing profitable arbitrage opportunities in real time. Users configure preferred exchanges and assets, set profit thresholds, and receive timely alerts through in-app notifications and supported channels. The dashboard showcases live metrics, recent opportunities, and a clear path to start, monitor, and refine scanning activity.

## Key Features
- Real-Time Scanner: Continuously analyzes supported exchanges to detect spreads and net profit after fees and slippage.
- Opportunities Hub: Ranked list of current opportunities with profit estimators (profit on $500, $1,000, $5,000, $10,000).
- Guided Configuration: Step-by-step wizard to select exchanges, choose assets, and set profit thresholds.
- Smart Notifications: In-app alerts tailored to your selections; optional email and Telegram channels for higher tiers.
- Risk Management Controls: Toggle inclusion of fees and slippage; set minimum profit thresholds.
- Activity Insights: At-a-glance stats including recent scans, last scan time, and total opportunities discovered.

## How It Works
1. Select Exchanges: Choose the venues you want the scanner to watch.
2. Choose Assets: Specify the crypto assets you care about.
3. Set Profit Thresholds: Define minimum net profit percentage to reduce noise.
4. Receive Alerts: Get notified when opportunities meet your criteria; view details, spreads, and estimated profits.
5. Review & Refine: Adjust preferences any time to widen or narrow the signal.

## Plans & Pricing
- Free: Designed for getting started with focused coverage and in-app notifications.
- Pro: Unlocks unlimited scanning and configuration, plus email alerts.
- Premium: Adds Telegram alerts along with all Pro benefits for power users and teams.

## Free Tier
- Exchanges: Select up to 2 exchanges; the interface prevents adding more once you hit the limit and shows an explanatory pop-up.
- Assets: Select up to 3 crypto assets; adding beyond the limit is blocked with a clear pop-up explaining the restriction.
- Monthly Scan Notifications: Receive up to 500 scan-driven notifications per month; once the cap is reached, an in-app notice informs you and suggests upgrading for unlimited alerts.
- Notification Channels: In-app notifications included; other channels require a higher tier.
- Strict Filtering: Alerts are only generated for the exchanges and assets you selected, ensuring relevance and control.

## Pro Tier
- Exchanges & Assets: Unlimited selections to tailor scanning across the entire supported universe.
- Monthly Scan Notifications: Unlimited scan-driven notifications for continuous monitoring.
- Notification Channels: In-app plus email notifications to stay informed on and off the platform.
- Advanced Flexibility: Ideal for users who want broader coverage without restrictions.

## Premium Tier
- Everything in Pro: Unlimited exchanges, assets, and scan notifications.
- Notification Channels: In-app, email, and Telegram alerts for the fastest delivery and extended reach.
- Best for Teams: Robust multi-channel communication for fast-moving workflows.

## Notifications
- In-App: Clean, actionable alerts with titles, messages, and quick context on profit estimates.
- Email (Pro and Premium): Deliver key opportunities to your inbox for off-platform access.
- Telegram (Premium): Near-instant alerts via bot messaging for high-momentum users.
- Smart Limits: Free tier caps notification volume to 500 per month; friendly notices explain when limits are reached.

## Getting Started
1. Create an Account: Sign up and log in.
2. Configure Scanner: Select exchanges and assets, then set your minimum net profit threshold.
3. Explore Opportunities: Visit the Opportunities Hub for ranked, current opportunities.
4. Tune Notifications: Adjust channels and preferences (additional channels require higher tiers).
5. Upgrade Anytime: Move to Pro or Premium to unlock unlimited scanning and additional notification channels.
